Discover Quick Facts

Essential information about our chosen destinations.

Where Is It?

Marsa Alam: Located on Egypt's Red Sea coast in the southeast.

St. Catherine: Located in the Sinai Peninsula, home to St. Catherine's Monastery.

Why Is It Famous?

Marsa Alam: Famous for beautiful Red Sea beaches, coral reefs, diving, snorkeling, and marine life.

St. Catherine: Famous for Mount Sinai, St. Catherine’s Monastery, and religious and historical significance.

A Little History

Marsa Alam: Once known for fishing and gold mining, it later became famous for its beaches and coral reefs.

St. Catherine: A historic religious area linked to Prophet Moses, home to a famous 6th-century monastery.
